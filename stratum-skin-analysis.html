02 — Results

Most likely findings

Ranked by pattern match confidence, generated by the trained model — always confirm with a licensed clinician.

!

This is not a medical device. Stratum is a pattern-recognition demo, not a diagnostic tool cleared for clinical use. Never delay care based on an app. If a spot is changing, bleeding, or worrying you, see a dermatologist or physician directly.

Process

Four passes, one photo

Each pass adds a layer to the read — the same order a dermatologist's eye moves through.

01

Capture

Photo is aligned and cropped to isolate the area of concern from surrounding skin.

02

Surface pass

Border shape, edge softness, and symmetry are mapped across the lesion.

03

Pigment pass

Color clusters and contrast against surrounding tissue are measured.

04

Ranked read

Layers are combined into a ranked list of likely findings with confidence scores.
